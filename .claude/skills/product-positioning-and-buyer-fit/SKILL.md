# Product Positioning and Buyer Fit

## Purpose
Translate implemented repository truth into a grounded view of product
positioning, buyer value, and UX/market fit.

## When to Use
- Reviewing homepage or pricing narrative
- Explaining why UVAI exists and what users pay for
- Comparing UVAI to generic builders or raw LLM usage
- Producing buyer, operator, or competitor assessments

## Required Evidence
- Anchor product claims to implemented code and current locked product policy
- Separate repo facts from market judgment
- Verify pricing and product boundaries from current repo authority files
- Use current public-site or public-competitor evidence for external judgments
- Avoid inventing capabilities not implemented in the repository

## Outputs
- Product-fit memo
- UX friction map
- Buyer-value summary
- Competitor framing
- Messaging recommendations separated from repo facts
